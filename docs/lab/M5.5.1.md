## Measured artifacts and performance limits

The tested systemd candidate was a stripped, statically linked ARM64 executable, approximately 7.3 MiB, with embedded frontend assets. Its SHA-256 was `9d4c6fdb148da30872246800f50cdc6338e94b078838eff64515b3f72ff77e93`. The earlier genuine-V7 browser run used candidate hash `71639a52776d53505f54329a95be5aa928d0ea40ae81e6550ed0977f80432f19`; the subsequent public/local socket limits were covered by unit tests and the final systemd candidate. Final source comments and documentation do not constitute another hardware result. Build hashes can differ with Go version and source-control build metadata.

On Mac-hosted Linux, 30-second scenarios measured approximately 0.20% idle CPU, 1.27% active single-terminal CPU and 1.96% for one viewer receiving 50 KiB/s. Maximum sampled RSS across the matrix was 16,500 KiB, and measured active echo p95 was about 1 ms. **Eight output observers used 6.83% of one core.** These are synthetic loopback TLS/WS results, not Pi measurements or browser-rendering latency. The benchmark environment shares OrbStack's kernel and does not reproduce a Pi 3B+'s available memory or processor speed.

Keep the existing ≤24/48/64 MiB idle/active/peak RSS, <1% idle CPU, <5% active mean CPU and <100 ms terminal latency targets. The historical Pi 5.55% observation and required sustained hardware acceptance remain open. Do not infer that a cached URL parse explains or fixes the original short workload.

## Recommended next physical step

Review and separately authorize the [stopped-machine scanner procedure](M5.5.1-switch-diagnostic.md), with exclusive GPIO ownership and the owner confirming each switch pattern. Capture raw stdout, process status, timestamps and the unchanged map before considering another launcher boot. The original scanner initializes directions/pull-ups and briefly selects matrix rows; this must be explicitly understood and authorized.

Do not deploy the optional service as a shortcut around the unresolved selection issue. After diagnosis, propose a fresh supervised coexistence test, then separately review any installed-service pilot and sustained resource measurements. The original physical-only experience remains the fallback, and neither service availability nor a reused serial port proves which guest is running.
